Smooth Air — Support

Smooth Air is a calm, offline companion for people who fly anyway. It tells you what is happening right now: each phase of the flight, the noises and sensations, and how much the cabin is really moving. It also has breathing and grounding exercises for the hard minutes.

Common questions

Does it work in airplane mode? Yes. Everything is on your phone. There is nothing to download in the air and no account to sign in to.

I bought Smooth Air on another device. Open the App Store, signed in with the same Apple Account, and download it again at no charge. There are no in-app purchases to restore.

Can I try the flight timeline before I fly? Yes. Settings → Practice flight plays a whole flight at 10× speed.

How accurate is the bump meter? It is an approximate reading from your phone's motion sensor, and it depends on where your phone is resting. It shows how much the cabin moved. It does not measure turbulence severity or safety.

Does Smooth Air forecast turbulence or track my flight? No. We don't guess your turbulence. We explain it while it happens.

Reminders didn't arrive. Check that notifications are allowed for Smooth Air in iOS Settings, and that your next flight is added in Prepare.

A fact looks wrong. Every fact shows its source and year. If something looks off, email us the card title and we'll check it.

Does the app collect data? No. See the privacy policy.

Not medical advice

Smooth Air is an educational and wellness app. It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for care from a qualified professional. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. If your fear of flying is severe or affects your daily life, consider talking to your doctor or a licensed mental-health professional.

If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, contact local emergency services. In the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). In the UK and Ireland, call Samaritans at 116 123.
