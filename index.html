Smooth Air — Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026

Smooth Air is a fear-of-flying companion that works entirely on your device, including in airplane mode. We built it to be private by design.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and makes no network requests. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your flight log (dates, routes, how each flight felt, what helped, your notes), calm-session history and settings are stored only on your device (and in your device backups, if you have them enabled). Deleting the app deletes this data.

Motion sensors

The bump meter reads your phone's accelerometer while its screen is open, to show approximately how much the cabin is moving. The readings stay on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Purchases

Smooth Air is a paid app. You buy it once through the App Store, which handles payment; we never see your payment details. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Notifications

If you turn on pre-flight reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No notification server is involved.

Share cards

Share cards are made on your device and only go where you choose to send them.

Health information

Smooth Air is educational and is not a medical device. It does not use HealthKit and does not send any health or wellbeing information anywhere.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
